## Project Brief

Design a modern, high-end marketing website for **Softgrid**, a software
development agency that builds **web apps, mobile apps, and agentic AI
solutions**. I need full **desktop** and **mobile** designs for every page
listed below. The site must look outstanding, cutting-edge, and highly
professional — this is a portfolio piece for the agency itself, so the
design quality IS the sales pitch.

**Brand tone:** Bold, confident, cutting-edge — not corporate-safe, not
playful/cute. Think premium tech/AI product, not generic "digital agency
with stock photos."

**Tagline (use as hero text):** "We Build Software That Thinks."

---

## 1. Visual Direction

**Overall aesthetic:** Dark-themed, premium SaaS/tech product design —
similar in spirit to the modern wave of AI/dev-tool sites (Linear, Vercel,
Stripe, OpenAI, Anthropic-style product pages). Not literal copies —
use this only as a reference point for the *category* of design: dark
backgrounds, gradient accent lighting, generous whitespace, confident
large typography, subtle motion.

**Mood:** Futuristic but clean. Technical but not cold. Should feel like
"a team that builds intelligent software," not "a template agency site."

**Avoid:** Generic stock photography of people in blazers shaking hands,
cliché "gear/circuit board" AI imagery, boxy Bootstrap-default layouts,
cluttered layouts, more than 2 accent colors, comic/playful illustration
styles.

---

## 2. Color Palette

- **Background (primary):** near-black, `#0A0A0F` or `#0B0D12`
- **Background (secondary/section alternation):** slightly lifted dark,
  `#12141C`
- **Primary accent (brand):** electric indigo/violet, `#6C5CE7` –
  `#7C5CFF` range
- **Secondary accent:** cyan/teal, `#22D3EE` — used for AI/tech highlights,
  gradients, and to differentiate the "Agentic AI" service from the other
  two
- **Gradient use:** violet → cyan diagonal gradients for hero glow effects,
  CTA buttons, section dividers, and accent lines. Use sparingly —
  gradients should feel like lighting, not decoration on every element.
- **Text:** off-white `#F5F5F7` for headings, muted gray `#9CA3AF` for
  body copy, dimmer gray `#6B7280` for captions/metadata
- **Borders/dividers:** low-opacity white, `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`

**Optional:** support a light mode toggle if your tool can do it easily,
but dark mode is the primary/default design — don't compromise the dark
design to accommodate light mode.

---

## 3. Typography

- **Headings:** modern geometric/grotesk sans-serif with a distinctive
  display weight — e.g. Inter, Geist, Satoshi, or General Sans. Large,
  confident sizing (hero H1 at 64-88px desktop, 36-44px mobile). Tight
  letter-spacing on large type.
- **Body:** same family or a clean pairing, 16-18px base, comfortable
  line-height (1.5-1.6) for readability on dark backgrounds.
- **Hierarchy:** strong size/weight contrast between H1/H2/body — this
  site should feel confidently typographic, not timid.

---

## 4. Layout & Component Style

- **Grid:** 12-column, generous side margins (96-120px desktop, 20-24px
  mobile), max content width ~1280-1440px, centered.
- **Corners:** large rounded corners on cards/buttons (16-24px radius) —
  soft, modern, not sharp/boxy.
- **Shadows/glow:** prefer soft colored glows (violet/cyan) over hard
  drop shadows. Feature cards can use subtle glassmorphism (semi-transparent
  background + backdrop blur + thin border).
- **Buttons:** primary CTA = solid or gradient-filled pill/rounded button
  with a soft glow on hover; secondary CTA = outlined/ghost button.
- **Motion:** subtle scroll-triggered fade/slide-in on sections, hover
  states with gentle scale/glow — nothing bouncy or slow. Motion should
  feel precise, not playful.
- **Imagery:** abstract 3D gradient shapes/orbs, isometric or wireframe
  device mockups showing actual product screens (web/mobile UI), particle
  or mesh-gradient backgrounds behind the hero. No stock photography of
  people.
- **Iconography:** thin-line, consistent-weight icon set (e.g. Phosphor,
  Lucide style) — not filled/cartoon icons.

## 5. Responsive Requirements

- **Desktop:** design at 1440px width (primary), should also hold up at

- **Mobile:** design at 390px width (iPhone-standard). Single-column

  process/timeline sections become vertical on mobile.
