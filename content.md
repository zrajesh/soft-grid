### Case Study 1 — BooksToHome
**Card summary (for Home page):**
An online bookstore platform for the Indian market — built for speed,
affordability, and a smooth checkout.

**Full case study:**

*The problem:* Bring an affordable, high-volume online bookstore to market
with a checkout experience that removes friction for Indian buyers —
Cash-on-Delivery, prepaid incentives, and fast regional shipping all had to
work together.

*What we built:* A full e-commerce platform — product catalog with
filtering and search, cart and wishlist, coupon/discount engine, order
tracking, and a COD + prepaid-discount checkout flow. Bulk-order inquiries
route through WhatsApp, and reviews sync with Google.

*Key features shipped:*
- Full storefront: catalog, search, filtering, wishlist, cart
- Checkout supporting COD (with handling fee logic) and prepaid discounts
- Coupon/discount code system
- Order tracking and account/order history
- Bulk-order inquiry channel (WhatsApp integration)
- Customer review system (Google-integrated)

*Role / outcome:* Built end-to-end by Softgrid — storefront, checkout, and
order management. Live and generating ₹20 Lakh+ in monthly revenue.

*Live at:* bookstohome.in

---

### Case Study 2 — LingoTree
**Card summary (for Home page):**
An AI-powered English learning app for beginners and intermediate
learners — built around a personalized roadmap and live AI conversation
practice.

**Full case study:**

*The problem:* Most English-learning apps stop at vocabulary drills and
grammar quizzes — they don't build real speaking confidence, and they
don't adapt to where a learner actually is.

*What we built:* LingoTree pairs a personalized, AI-driven learning
roadmap with a standout AI Calling feature — learners practice real
conversations with multiple AI personalities, building communication
skills beyond flashcards. Built for beginner and intermediate English
learners.

*Key features shipped:*
- Personalized AI-driven learning roadmap
- AI Calling — live conversation practice with multiple AI personalities
- Beginner & intermediate learning tracks

*Role / outcome:* Built by Softgrid, published and live on Google Play.

*Live at:* Google Play — LingoTree
